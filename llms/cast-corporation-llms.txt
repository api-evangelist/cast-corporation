# Cast Corporation (Cast.app)

> Cast.app is an AI-agent platform that automates customer success and post-sales
> revenue operations. Its autonomous agents deliver personalized digital business
> reviews, grounded answers, onboarding, adoption, renewal-influence, and referral
> workflows across email, in-app, and chat in 17 languages, connecting natively to
> 60+ systems including Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, Totango, and data warehouses.
> This is an API Evangelist network profile; Cast does not publish a public developer API.
